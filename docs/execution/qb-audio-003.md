## Deviations

No product implementation deviations. The November 4 review identifies a valid design contradiction: v3's M-only timeout can close healthy audio during NVENC initialization or an ordinary production pause. Versioned v4 corrects that rule before implementation and simplifies the diagnostics; v1-v3 and prior evidence remain unchanged. Endpoint acquisition failures remain isolated upstream of healthy PCM delivery; arbitrary corrupted pipe input or a failed shared FFmpeg child cannot be promised video survival. Strict media validation remains mandatory.

## Decisions

- The linked v4 ExecPlan is the approved implementation authority. Keep v1-v3 unchanged. The user approved implementation; feature status is in-progress.
- Endpoint loss, unusual timestamps, unavailable capture and capture-owner cleanup are independent degradations. Keep their fallback upstream of a healthy PCM pump; they never cancel an in-flight write. Managed complete-prefix EOF has demonstrated limited support. Actual shared mux/storage/video/container failures remain terminal, including corruption caused by malformed audio input.
- Reuse the obtained transport facts and implement the resolved contract next. Extend only focused production regressions; do not restart architecture review or treat transport evidence as proof of actual WASAPI sound/synchronization.
- Bound reserved/emitted audio by `max(24000, 800*M + 24000)` at 48 kHz/60 FPS, with M from existing mux progress. Keep bootstrap independent of M. Managed EOF requires cap active, unchanged M and the same video sink operation continuously pending for one second; signal immediately around buffered I/O and change generation on entry/exit. No pending write means no escape timer. Existing startup/video watchdogs stay authoritative.
- The 500-ms stop deadline includes pending writes; retain outstanding I/O resources safely and independently of video. Accept at most 542.7 ms total positive audio overhang including the codec allowance as a product tradeoff, with video-authoritative replay/export endpoints checked on the already-required early-stop artifact.
- Preserve one-second pre-epoch capture without awaiting endpoint initialization. Use fixed D=250 ms, checked by real-device observation rather than fitted to it. Keep fill by reason, scalar late-valid/non-SILENT-late counts and maximum lateness; no historical interval ledger or packet-arrival guarantee. Short A/V markers do not establish long-duration drift performance.
- Reject an obsolete/invalid audio source nonfatally: actionable warning and video-only session, never recording startup failure or process suppression. Intentional silent/none remains explicit.
- Keep recovery's exact validated terminal timeline/receipt comparison. Normal optional-AAC capture does not authorize a recovered file to lose a previously validated audio track; no second persisted media authority is needed.
- No new UI lifecycle or replay/export controls. This repair reports audio outcome through diagnostics, transition warnings and a bounded saved summary.
- Preserve unrelated modified/untracked work, especially `recorder/examples/native_nvenc_probe.rs`, audio investigation tools/results, the performance checkpoint and recorder review. Only QB-AUDIO-003 may change in the existing roadmap.
